# Changelog

## v1.2.0 — Daily cardio after weights (2026-11-07)

- **Cardio card on Train, every day:** Treadmill or Elliptical toggle (coach pick dotted: elliptical on back and leg days, easier on tired knees), with the machine animation.
- **Minute by minute:** a table of warm-up, main part and cool-down with time, speed (km/h, mph conversion) and incline, or elliptical resistance level. How it feels for each part.
- **Steps up with the plan:** weeks 1-3 incline walk 4.5 km/h at 4% (12 min), weeks 4-7 4.8 km/h at 6% (15 min), week 8 on 2-min hills at 5.0 km/h and 8% (6 rounds). Elliptical: level 3, then 5, then 7/5 intervals. Leg days are shorter. Deload lighter. Ramadan: flat and easy, after iftar only. Saturday 30-min walk. Sunday optional easy walk.
- **Guided timer:** full screen, counts each part down, says the next speed, incline or level, 3-2-1 beeps, pause, screen stays on. Logs a cardio workout with the real minutes; or "Mark done".
- **Safety:** easy-cardio heart-rate band from her birth year (talk test first), "spotter not needed", step-by-step safe use of each machine (safety key, getting on and off), stop signs.

## v1.1.0 — Workout player, safety notes, verified animations (2026-11-06)

- **Start workout:** full-screen player, one set at a time: big animation, set dots, rest countdown (+15 s / skip), 3-2-1 beeps, voice cues (device voice; Urdu when an Urdu voice is installed), screen stays on. Logs the top set like the card.
- **Safety on every exercise:** spotter needed or not (machine / cable / Smith / dumbbells) and a light starting weight. Every plan exercise and alternative has its own entry (tested). A stop-for-pain line on Train.
- **Swaps redone:** reason segment, three quick options with thumbnails, and "See all" — a sheet of cards with the animation, a Hindi video and "Use this". A swapped exercise shows its own animation, video and log.
- **Animations checked one by one:** wrong matches fixed (bench push-up, triceps kickback, dumbbell overhead extension, side bend, calf raises, single-leg bridge…) or removed where the library has no true match (shoulder press machine, rear delt machine, hip thrust machine, floor press…). A video button stays on all of them. GitHub fallback when the CDN refuses a file.
- **Warm-up and cool-down demos:** each row has a thumbnail; tap for the animation and a Hindi video.
- **Tiny credit:** "© Gym visual" now sits small and faint in the corner of each animation.
- **Day tabs:** full names (Mon · Chest & Triceps, Tue · Back & Biceps, Thu · Shoulders & Abs, Fri · Full body & Glutes).
- **App tour** on first open (7 slides, English/Urdu); Settings → Take the tour.

## v1.0.0 — Fit Mamma (2026-11-06)

First release, forked from Project 85 v0.15.0.

- New name, icon (heart + trend line) and warm light theme with larger text.
- Her plan to Eid (10 Mar 2027): 1,500 kcal / 110 g protein, steps 6k → 8k.

- Celebration stickers: workout done, new best, new lowest weight, each kg lost, goal.
